The origins

Our school was born from the passion of a group of DJs and producers who wanted to share their experience. The idea was to create a school where aspiring artists could learn directly from professionals, in a practical and inspiring environment.

Euphonic today

Today, our school is a reference point for DJs and producers of all levels. From fully equipped classrooms to recording studios, the school provides a creative environment where each student can create and develop their own style, alongside a team of experienced instructors.

Marco Masone

Deejay

Faculty:

Djing

Curriculum

Marco Masone is a professional DJ and instructor who has performed at numerous national and international events and clubs. He started his musical journey as a self-taught artist, later refining his skills through advanced courses in mixing and music production. Over the years, Marco has developed a deep understanding of DJing techniques, from vinyl to digital, and is highly skilled in the latest technologies, such as DJ controllers and software. Currently, he teaches his students best practices in mixing, using equipment, and creating DJ sets, providing them with the tools to pursue a career in the world of electronic music.

Roberto Costa

Sound Design

Faculty:

Sound Synthesis & Sound design

Curriculum

Roberto Costa is an experienced sound designer and the instructor of the "Sound Synthesis & Sound Design" Master's course. With an academic background in sound engineering from the Accademia del Suono in Milan, and a career that has seen him collaborate with production studios and internationally renowned artists, Roberto specializes in creating unique sounds and in the advanced use of both analog and digital synthesizers. His expertise spans sound design for film and video games, as well as music production, with a particular focus on innovation in sound synthesis. He teaches students sound design techniques, synthesizer programming, and audio post-production, training professionals who are capable of shaping and manipulating sound in a creative and effective way.

Ilaria De Martino

Producer

Faculty:

Music production

Curriculum

Ilaria De Martino is a producer with solid experience in the electronic and pop music sectors, currently teaching music production at our school. After studying composition and production at the "National Academy of Music Production" in Manchester, she worked as an independent producer, collaborating with both national and international artists and creating projects for record labels and media companies such as "AV Music Production" and "BeatHarmony Production." Her experience includes creating original tracks, arranging music, and professional mixing. In the classroom, Ilaria teaches music production techniques, the use of DAW (Digital Audio Workstation) software, and managing the entire creative process, helping her students develop their own style and prepare for the music industry.

Giovanni Iodice

Producer

Faculty:

Music production

Curriculum

Giovanni Iodice is an experienced producer and instructor of "Music Production" at our school. He holds a degree in "Sound Techniques" from the Conservatory of Naples and later specialized in music production with a Master's in "Sound Engineering" in London. Throughout his career, he has worked both as an independent producer and as a collaborator with high-level recording studios, producing albums for nationally and internationally recognized artists. In addition to his work as a producer, Giovanni is the author of the book "The Art of Music Production", a technical manual that explores the stages of music creation, from recording techniques to mixing and mastering, highly regarded in the industry. Currently, in his teaching, he focuses on advanced DAW use, creative arranging, and the importance of sound identity for emerging producers.

João Pedro Muniz

Deejay

Faculty:

DJing

Curriculum

João Pedro Muniz is a DJing instructor with a solid academic and professional background. He holds a degree in Music Production and Sound Engineering from the Universidade de São Paulo, where he focused on mixing techniques and composition. After his studies, he began a career as a DJ, performing at clubs and festivals in Brazil and Europe, developing a distinctive style that blends various musical genres. He has collaborated with artists and producers, contributing to musical projects and remixes. Currently, he teaches at our Euphonic Academy, where he trains his students in essential techniques such as beatmatching and the use of professional software. João is also the author of articles on the practice of DJing, supporting the growth of new talents in the music industry.

Ivan Esposito

Singer, guitarist, and pianist

Faculty:

Canto, chitarra e piano

Curriculum

Ivan Esposito is a vocal, guitar, and piano instructor at Euphonic Academy, with a strong academic background and a successful professional career. He studied Classical and Popular Singing at the Conservatory of Naples and earned a degree in Music and Composition in Milan. With a Master's in Vocal Techniques and Performance from the London School of Music, Ivan has gained experience as a singer, musician, and composer, collaborating with artists and bands both in Italy and abroad. His teaching focuses on integrating technique and creativity, guiding students through the fundamentals of singing, guitar, and piano, while helping them develop their own musical style. With his practical and inspiring approach, Ivan is a key figure for those looking to embark on a professional career in music
