Sandra Merola

Deejay

Biography

Sandra Merola is one of the most well-known female DJs in the contemporary music scene. Ambitious and with an innate musical talent, she attended Euphonic Academy starting at the age of 8, first joining the children's course and later the adult course during her high school years. Today, her music is heard in over 110 countries.

Artistic career

Sandra Merola began her music career in 2010 with the EP "God Forget Me". Her recent albums, "Dreams" and "Tomorrow", have achieved great international success, with tracks like "No Stars" and "Hear My Voice", which quickly became hits in clubs worldwide. Her presence at major international festivals, including Tomorrowland, Ultra Music Festival, and Creamfields, has solidified her status as one of the most sought-after DJs in the electronic scene.

She says about us

"Attending Euphonic Academy was the best decision of my life! Not only did it give me the opportunity to grow professionally, but it also allowed me to meet amazing people who are still a part of my life today, with whom I have developed both professional and personal connections."
