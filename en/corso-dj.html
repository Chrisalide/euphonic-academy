Course Information

Find answers to the most common questions about our course here

Who is the course for?

The course is aimed at individuals aged 18 to 70 who wish to learn the art of DJing, regardless of their educational background or previous experience level. However, it is required that participants are not engaged in work or school activities during the course days and hours, as attendance is mandatory.

What to expect from this course

During this course, you will have the opportunity to become a true professional DJ through both theoretical and practical lessons, seminars, and workshops. Additionally, throughout the program, students will have the chance to meet and collaborate with some of the most important artists in the industry. At the end of the course, the experience and skills gained will enable immediate entry into the workforce.

What is included in the price

  • State-of-the-art classrooms and equipment available to the student for the entire duration of the course
  • Workshops, seminars, and various educational outings
  • Canteen service and library available at any time of the day
  • Development, production, and promotion of the first music albums
  • Internationally recognized certification

Exam and Certification

At the end of each academic year, the student will be required to undergo both practical and theoretical evaluation tests. If the results are negative, the student will have the opportunity to make up for it in the following year through extracurricular courses, held outside regular class hours. After completing these supplementary courses, the student will again undergo an evaluation exam. At the end of the final academic year, the student must pass a final exam in front of a panel, consisting of a practical test and an interview where they will showcase the work completed throughout the academic program. Upon successful completion, the student will receive a certification that is valid internationally.

How to enroll

To enroll, you need to fill out the contact form in the "Contact" section of our website and specify which course you are interested in. Afterward, one of our staff members will contact you to schedule an introductory meeting. It is absolutely necessary, for enrollment purposes, to bring a valid ID for the student (or for the guardian if the student is a minor) and the completed enrollment form with a revenue stamp (which can be downloaded at the top of the page).
